🛡️ The False-Alarm Guard

Soft pressure signs (like "pay today") alone are strictly capped under 60 points. High Risk is reserved only for messages containing at least one Hard Danger sign (lookalike links, OTP theft, fake senders).

The average cyber fraud victim in India loses between ₹25,000 to ₹1,50,000. Preventing even a single incident saves a family more than years of security subscriptions.
